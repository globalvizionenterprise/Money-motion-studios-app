Privacy Policy

GLOBAL VIZION ENTERPRISE — home of MONEY MOTION STUDIOS (an affiliate)
Last updated: November 3, 2026

This policy explains what information the GLOBAL VIZION ENTERPRISE app and website collect, how it is used, and the choices you have. The app is a booking and information app for MONEY MOTION STUDIOS, a recording studio in Brooklyn, NY.

Information we collect

  • Booking information. When you book a studio session, we collect your name, phone number, email address, the service and time you choose, and any notes you add (for example, how many songs). This is stored in our booking database (Supabase) so we can confirm, run, and manage your session.
  • AI chat messages. If you use the in-app AI Chat, the messages you type are sent to our chat provider (OpenRouter) and processed by AI models to generate a reply. Please do not put sensitive personal information in chat messages.
  • Basic technical data. The app is delivered over the web; the hosting and database providers may process standard technical data such as IP address and browser type as part of providing the service.

What we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect or store payment card or bank details. Booking-fee payments happen inside your own Cash App, Venmo, or Chime app — never inside this app.
  • We do not run advertising, advertising trackers, or analytics profiles.
  • We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

How we use your information

  • To confirm and manage your studio booking, including contacting you about your session.
  • To answer questions you send through the AI Chat.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the app.

Sharing

We share booking information only with the service providers that run the app (hosting and database) and only as needed to operate it. AI chat messages are processed by OpenRouter and the AI model providers it routes to, solely to return your answer. We may disclose information if required by law.

Data retention and deletion

Booking records are kept for as long as needed for studio business records. You can ask us to delete your personal information at any time by emailing littmobstudios@gmail.com and we will delete it unless we must keep it for legal or accounting reasons.

Children

This app is for booking professional studio services and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. No system is perfectly secure, but we use reputable providers and limit access to booking records to the studio team.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
